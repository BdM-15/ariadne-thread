> **Entity type:** `concept`

Structured decision process for opportunity qualification. Key criteria: (1) Strategic fit - aligns with corporate capabilities and growth strategy, (2) Customer relationship - existing intimacy and access for shaping, (3) Competitive position - realistic chance of winning vs. field, (4) Resource availability - can staff pursuit and execution, (5) Contract profitability - acceptable terms and pricing opportunity, (6) Pwin assessment - probability of win exceeds threshold (typically 40-50%). Gate review typically requires executive approval. Decline decisions should be documented for lessons learned. Avoid 'hope' bids consuming B&P resources.
