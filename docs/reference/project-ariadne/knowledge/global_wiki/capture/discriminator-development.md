> **Entity type:** `strategic_theme`

Discriminators are unique strengths that differentiate from competitors. Effective discriminators are: (1) Customer-valued - addresses hot buttons or evaluation criteria, (2) Differentiating - competitors cannot easily claim same, (3) Provable - supported by evidence (past performance, certifications, metrics), (4) Believable - credible given your organization's capabilities. Discriminator sources: proprietary technology, unique past performance, key personnel, teaming arrangements, innovative approaches, price advantages. Validate discriminators through Black Hat review - if competitors can neutralize, it's not a true discriminator. Each proposal should have 3-5 strong discriminators.
